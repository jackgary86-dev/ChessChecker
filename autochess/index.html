⚔ Chess vs Checkers

Two armies in their classic starting ranks, one board, four random blocked tiles. Chess fights as fierce individuals; Checkers fights as one defensive hive. Press Fight and watch it resolve.

Drafting: click one of your units, then click another of your own units to swap their positions. Rearrange as much as you like, then press Start Battle! when ready.

Chess: 16 Checkers: 12
Chess loses instantly if its High King falls — Checkers loses when every unit is down. A Footsoldier or Draughtsman reaching the far row is promoted to Veteran. Every Checkers unit reduces incoming damage by 1 for each adjacent living ally: the hive mind defends together.
